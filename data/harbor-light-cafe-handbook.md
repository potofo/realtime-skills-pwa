# Harbor Light Cafe — Staff Handbook

Harbor Light Cafe is a fictional neighborhood cafe located at 12 Pier Road, Seabrook. It opened in 2019 and seats 40 guests.

## Opening Hours

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday – Friday | 7:00 – 18:00 |
| Saturday | 8:00 – 20:00 |
| Sunday | 8:00 – 16:00 |
| Public holidays | Closed |

The cafe stays open until 20:00 on Saturdays because of the evening acoustic music night, which starts at 18:30.

## Signature Menu

- **Lighthouse Latte** — double espresso, steamed oat milk, and a touch of honey. $5.20.
- **Foghorn Cold Brew** — brewed for 18 hours, served over a single large ice cube. $4.80.
- **Tide Pool Tea** — green tea with yuzu and mint. $4.00.
- **Harbor Scone** — baked every morning at 6:00; flavors rotate between blueberry, lemon, and cheddar-chive. $3.50.
- **Captain's Sandwich** — smoked salmon, cream cheese, capers, and dill on rye. $9.50.

Gluten-free bread is available for any sandwich for an extra $1.00. All milk drinks can be made with whole milk, oat milk, or soy milk at no extra charge.

## Loyalty Program

Guests receive one stamp per drink. Ten stamps earn a free drink of any size. Stamps expire 12 months after the first stamp on the card.

## Staff Policies

- Shifts are scheduled two weeks in advance and posted every other Friday.
- Staff must request shift swaps at least 48 hours ahead through the manager on duty.
- Each staff member receives one free drink per shift and a 30% discount on food.
- Breaks: 15 minutes for shifts of 4–6 hours; 30 minutes for shifts longer than 6 hours.
- Uniform: black apron (provided), closed-toe shoes, hair tied back.

## Opening Checklist

1. Turn on the espresso machine and let it heat for 20 minutes.
2. Calibrate the grinder: a double shot should take 26–30 seconds.
3. Check fridge temperatures (must be 1–4 °C) and record them in the log.
4. Put out the day's scones by 7:00.

## Closing Checklist

1. Backflush the espresso machine with cleaner.
2. Wipe down all counters and sanitize the milk pitchers.
3. Count the cash drawer and record the total.
4. Lock the back door and set the alarm (code is held by the manager).
