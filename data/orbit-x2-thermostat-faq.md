# Orbit X2 Smart Thermostat — Frequently Asked Questions

The Orbit X2 is a fictional Wi-Fi thermostat made by the fictional company Brightfield Home.

## Specifications

- Display: 2.4-inch color touchscreen
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, Bluetooth Low Energy for setup
- Power: 24 V C-wire, or the included power adapter for homes without a C-wire
- Temperature range: 5 °C to 35 °C, adjustable in 0.5 °C steps
- Sensors: temperature, humidity, and motion
- Warranty: 3 years

## Setup

**How long does installation take?**
Most customers finish in about 30 minutes. The app guides you through photographing your old wiring before you remove the old thermostat.

**My home has no C-wire. Can I still use the Orbit X2?**
Yes. Use the included power adapter, which connects at the furnace. Do not run the thermostat on batteries; it has no battery compartment.

**How do I connect it to Wi-Fi?**
Open the Brightfield Home app, tap "Add Device", and hold the phone near the thermostat. The app transfers the Wi-Fi settings over Bluetooth.

## Features

**What is Eco Mode?**
Eco Mode lowers heating by 2 °C (or raises cooling by 2 °C) when the motion sensor detects no one at home for 90 minutes. Brightfield Home estimates savings of 8–12% on heating bills.

**Can I set a schedule?**
Yes. You can create up to 6 temperature periods per day, with different schedules for each day of the week.

**Does it work with voice assistants?**
The Orbit X2 supports the Matter standard, so it works with any Matter-compatible voice assistant or smart home hub.

**Does it show energy reports?**
A monthly energy report is sent to the app on the first day of each month. It compares your heating and cooling hours with the previous month.

## Troubleshooting

**The screen is blank.**
Check that the circuit breaker for the heating system is on. If you use the power adapter, check that it is firmly connected at the furnace.

**The thermostat shows error E3.**
E3 means the Wi-Fi connection was lost. Restart your router, then hold the thermostat's outer ring for 10 seconds to restart the thermostat.

**The thermostat shows error E7.**
E7 means the heating system did not respond. Turn off power to the system, check the wiring against the photos you took during setup, and contact support if the error continues.

**How do I factory reset it?**
Open Settings > Reset > Factory Reset on the thermostat screen, then confirm twice. All schedules and Wi-Fi settings will be erased.
