GREENWAY VALLEY TRAILS GUIDE
============================

Greenway Valley is a fictional nature park with four marked trails. The park is open from sunrise to sunset every day. Entry is free; parking costs $3 per day.

TRAIL 1: Meadow Loop (easy)
- Length: 2.5 km, round trip
- Estimated time: 45 minutes
- Elevation gain: 20 m
- Surface: flat gravel path, suitable for strollers and wheelchairs
- Highlights: wildflower fields in May and June, a bird-watching hut near the pond

TRAIL 2: Cedar Ridge Trail (moderate)
- Length: 6 km, round trip
- Estimated time: 2 hours
- Elevation gain: 250 m
- Surface: dirt path with some rocky sections
- Highlights: a viewpoint at the top with a view of the whole valley; best at sunset

TRAIL 3: Falls Trail (moderate)
- Length: 4 km, one way (return by the same path)
- Estimated time: 1.5 hours one way
- Elevation gain: 150 m
- Surface: wooden boardwalk and dirt path; can be slippery after rain
- Highlights: Silver Thread Falls, a 30 m waterfall; the path closes in winter (December to February) because of ice

TRAIL 4: Summit Challenge (hard)
- Length: 11 km, round trip
- Estimated time: 5 hours
- Elevation gain: 720 m
- Surface: steep, rocky path with one section using chains
- Highlights: the summit of Mount Alder (1,180 m)
- Note: not recommended for children under 12. Start before 10:00 so you return before sunset.

RULES
- Dogs are allowed on all trails except the Falls Trail, and must be kept on a leash.
- Camping and campfires are not allowed in the park.
- Bicycles are allowed only on the Meadow Loop.
- Please take all trash home; there are no trash bins on the trails.

FACILITIES
- Visitor center at the main entrance: maps, restrooms, drinking water, and a small shop. Open 9:00 to 17:00.
- Restrooms are also available at the Meadow Loop pond and at the start of the Summit Challenge.
- There is no mobile phone signal on most of the Summit Challenge.

EMERGENCIES
Call the park ranger station at 555-0142. Emergency phones are located at the Cedar Ridge viewpoint and halfway up the Summit Challenge.
